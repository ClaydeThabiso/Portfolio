Risk-Aware Fleet Monitoring

The Challenge

Logistics companies face significant asset losses due to drivers unknowingly navigating into localized high-risk areas that standard GPS systems do not flag.

The Engineering Solution

Architected the foundational logic for a full-stack application utilizing the PERN stack. By integrating PostGIS with mapping APIs, the system utilizes clustering algorithms (like DBSCAN/K-Means) on historical incident data to define spatial "risk geofences." The backend continually monitors active truck coordinates against these polygons to trigger preventative alerts.
